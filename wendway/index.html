Wendway

A one-line puzzle game for iPhone.

Start on the dot. Drag one continuous line so it covers every open tile exactly once. Each puzzle is a small grid, and the rules change as you go.

150 levels in 5 worlds

Each world has 30 levels and adds one new rule.

  1. 1. Paper: some tiles are missing (gaps).
  2. 2. Hedge: walls stand between some tiles.
  3. 3. Harbor: the line must end on the flag.
  4. 4. Current: some tiles are one-way, shown by arrows.
  5. 5. Lantern: warp tiles jump the line to their twin.

Stuck?

You start with 3 hints. A hint shows the next few correct steps. You can get more hints in the shop, or by watching a short ad.

Ads and purchases

Wendway is free to play. Full-screen ads appear between some levels. A one-time purchase called Remove Ads turns those off. Hint packs are also sold in the shop. Purchases are handled by Apple.
