Restore a purchase

If you bought Remove Ads and it is missing after you reinstall or change phones:

  1. Open Wendway and go to Shop or Settings.
  2. Tap Restore Purchases.
  3. Wait a moment. Remove Ads should return.

Purchases are handled by Apple. Use the Apple ID you bought the item with.

Hint packs are consumable, so Restore Purchases does not bring them back. Your hint balance is kept on your device only, so a reinstall or a new phone starts with the 3 starting hints.

Hints

You start with 3 hints. A hint costs 1 hint from your balance and shows the next few correct steps. When your balance is 0, the hint sheet lets you watch a short ad for 1 hint, or open the shop.

Ads

Full-screen ads may appear between some levels. To turn them off, buy Remove Ads in the shop. Where it is offered, you can also use Privacy choices in Settings to change your ad consent. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Report an inappropriate ad

If you see an ad in Wendway that is inappropriate, offensive, misleading, or not suitable for the app's age rating, please tell us. Send an email to info@dunewater.net with the following:

  • A screenshot of the ad. Include the whole screen if you can.
  • The date and time you saw it, including your time zone.
  • The level you were on, if you remember it.
  • What you think is wrong with the ad, in a sentence or two.

You can also reach this section from the Report an ad link in Settings.

Ads in Wendway are shown by Google AdMob. Google's own ad controls and policies also apply to the ad itself.
