Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Wendway game does with information. Wendway is published by Dune Water Studio LLC. It is an iPhone game with no accounts.

What stays on your device

The game keeps your progress on your iPhone. This includes which levels you solved, your hint count, and your sound and haptics settings. The game does not send this progress to us or to anyone else. If you delete Wendway, this data is deleted with it.

Google AdMob ads

Wendway shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad SDK (software that runs inside the app) may collect the following information when it shows and measures ads:

  • Device identifiers. This includes the advertising identifier on your iPhone, when you allow it, and other identifiers that work only inside this app.
  • Advertising data. For example, which ads you have seen.
  • Product interactions. For example, taps and video views on an ad.
  • Coarse location. This is an approximate location, worked out from the IP address your phone uses on the internet. Wendway does not ask for your precise location.
  • Crash and performance data. Google uses this to find and fix problems with the SDK. Google may also use it for advertising and analytics.

Google uses this information to show ads and to measure how ads perform. Google may use some of it for its own advertising and analytics. Google's own description is on its developer pages: AdMob iOS data disclosure and AdMob iOS privacy. Google's data handling is covered by Google's own policies, not by this one.

Your choices about ads and tracking

  • Consent form. Where the law requires it, a consent form from Google appears the first time you open Wendway. It lets you choose how ads are shown to you.
  • Tracking permission. After that, iPhone asks whether Wendway may track your activity across other apps and websites. This question is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and the choice is yours. You can change it at any time in your iPhone settings.
  • If you decline. The game works the same whichever answer you give. If you decline tracking, the advertising identifier on your iPhone is not used for the ads you see. Where the law requires your consent and you do not give it, Wendway may not show ads at all.
  • Privacy choices. The Settings screen in Wendway has a Privacy choices row. Where Google provides it, this reopens the consent form so you can change your answer.
  • Remove Ads. You can buy Remove Ads once. It turns off full-screen ads between levels. Buying it does not require you to agree to any tracking.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Wendway does not see or store your payment details. Wendway receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made, so it can unlock the item. Use Restore Purchases in the shop or in Settings to get back Remove Ads after you reinstall. Hint packs are consumable, so Restore Purchases does not bring them back, and your hint balance is kept on your device only.

Children

Wendway is not directed at children under 13, and it is not in Apple's Kids category. The game itself does not ask for personal information. Ads are shown by Google, as described above. If you are a parent and you think your child has given information through an ad, contact Google through the links above and contact us at the address below.

How long data is kept

Progress is kept on your device until you delete Wendway. We do not keep any copy of it. Data that Google collects through ads is kept under Google's policies. Apple keeps its own purchase records under Apple's policies.

Deleting your data and your choices

Dune Water Studio LLC holds no copy of your progress, hint balance or settings. They live on your iPhone, and deleting Wendway deletes them. We hold no account for you, so there is nothing else for us to delete.

The advertising data described above is collected by Google, not by us. Google, not Dune Water Studio LLC, decides how it is kept and deleted, under Google's own policies (the Google links above describe the data). To stop it going forward, change your answers: use the Privacy choices row in Wendway Settings where it is offered, and the tracking setting in your iPhone settings. You can also email us at info@dunewater.net with a question or a request about your data, and we will tell you where to take it.

Apple's privacy information

Apple shows a summary of the app's privacy practices on the App Store page. Apple's definitions of these categories are on its App privacy details page.
